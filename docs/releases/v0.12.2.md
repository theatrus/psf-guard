# v0.12.2

PSF Guard 0.12.2 fixes Windows desktop startup and stack previews missing from
the desktop Sky map.

## Fixed

- Completed mono and colour stacks appear inside their fields in **Sky**
  when **Stacks when zoomed** is on and the map is zoomed past 3×. The
  desktop app now loads these previews from its local server.
- The Windows desktop app finishes starting instead of remaining on
  **Checking access…**. Its local server keeps one socket across restarts.
- Previews no longer fail when the same frame is in two image folders, such
  as during a copy from an incoming folder to its final folder. PSF Guard
  reads the largest copy with matching capture headers. Files that contain
  different captures still show an error. See
  [Frames in more than one folder](../IMPORTING.md#frames-in-more-than-one-folder).
- Quality analysis catches a whole night or target shot at the wrong angle.
  A fresh solve more than 5° from the target's planned rotation is flagged
  **Rotation off plan**, capped at a score of 0.30 and left out of stack
  previews. Meridian flips still match the plan. See
  [astrometry quality grading](../ASTROMETRY_QUALITY.md#what-is-flagged).

## Upgrading

- No catalog migration or stack rebuild is needed for this fix. Existing
  completed stacks work after upgrading.
- If a target's plan changed after its frames were captured, set its planned
  rotation to the angle you want before analyzing their quality.
