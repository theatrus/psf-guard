## Changes

- **Nights** (early beta), a new view beside **Sky**, shows every rig across
  the coming nights: the clear dark hours, the Moon, and the plans each rig
  would shoot. Choose a night to see its schedule, a prediction of what
  Target Scheduler will shoot there and why it picks each target, or what
  Director would do. Mark a night available, not available, or clear only
  for some hours, for one rig or for every rig at a site; the schedules
  work around the marks. The Live table's new **Tonight** column links each
  rig to its night. See [Nights](../DIRECTOR.md#nights).
