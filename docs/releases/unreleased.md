## Changes
- Keep dewy hours out of night plans. A site's forecast now includes
  temperature, dew point and humidity. In **Settings › Presets › Sites**,
  enter a limit in **Not clear within**: hours whose air comes that many °C
  or closer to its dew point no longer count as clear. A night's timeline
  shows a dew band under the cloud band, and the legend lists the hours
  inside the limit. Like cloud, dew changes planning estimates, not the
  rig's safety controls.
