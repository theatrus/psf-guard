## Changes

- **New plan** now sits in the Library's toolbar and at the top of the
  header's project picker, so you can start a plan with no images or
  database. **Create plan** opens the plan on **Framing** with **Find a
  target** ready. Before, **New plan** was at the bottom of the Library, and
  saving left you in the list.
- A new plan offers **Add objective** beside its rigs. A rig joins a plan by
  shooting its objectives, so **Add a rig** and the rig switches on
  **Framing** stay disabled until the plan has one; before, only a tooltip
  and a grey line explained why.
