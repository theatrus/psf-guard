Made from existing PSF Guard panel stacks, mean-binned 4× per axis, then integrated on Seiza’s expanded canvas. The saved panel stacks represent 1,243 accepted exposures and 45 h 35 min across all six filters. Color jobs ran through PSF Guard’s tracked-job API. RGB-star and starless examples used licensed StarXTerminator. PSF Guard’s continuum-stage integration remains pending in the companion PRs.
